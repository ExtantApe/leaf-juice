LeafJuice banners: the square tile's colour

The palette is analogous greens (hues 75° to 97°) with grapefruit (11°) as the one complementary pop. The question is what the square tile should be.

TileEdge on creamEdge on darkGlass outline on tile
A. Pistachio, as now1.28 : 1, fails11.78 : 19.29 : 1
B. Pistachio with a sage ring on light3.70 : 111.78 : 1 (no ring needed)9.29 : 1
C. Grapefruit wash with a rind ring on light5.99 : 18.95 : 1 (no ring needed)8.69 : 1

3:1 is the minimum for a shape you need to see (WCAG 1.4.11). The text everywhere is far above 4.5:1. On C, the grapefruit wedge sits at 1.69:1 against the tile; its rind outline (4.38:1) keeps its shape. Solid grapefruit and juice-green tiles were measured too and dropped: their edges fail on cream (2.31 and 1.95), and the green one swallows the juice.
